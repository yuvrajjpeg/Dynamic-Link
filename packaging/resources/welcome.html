Dynamic Bridge

Edit a DaVinci Resolve shot in After Effects. Save, and the render returns to your timeline as a new take.

This installer adds:

  • Two scripts under Workspace → Scripts in Resolve
  • The bridge files in your user Library
  • A companion script under File → Scripts in After Effects, when it is installed

Requires: macOS, Python 3.9 or later, DaVinci Resolve 21 (Free, Studio or App Store) and After Effects.
